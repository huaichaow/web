Find the One Ring

One Ring to rule them all, One Ring to find them,
One Ring to bring them all, and in the darkness bind them.

The Ring has been lost among the drifting debris of a shattered world. Somewhere in this sphere of rubble a single golden band still glimmers. Seek it out and claim it before it finds a new master.

  • Drag / pan — turn your gaze left, right, up and down
  • Pinch or scroll — fly forward and backward
  • Tap / click the Ring — claim it when you are close enough

The golden glow at the edge of your vision grows warmer as you draw near.

Running in WebGL compatibility mode — WebGPU is not available on this device.
